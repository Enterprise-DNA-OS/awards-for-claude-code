# Awards evidence and privacy checks

Checked 11 October 2026. These checks are reminders for an operator to inspect evidence. They do not certify compliance, decide legal applicability or publish results.

| Check | Record rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Notice review | Flag entries without a recorded assessment of collection and notice obligations | NZ Privacy Act principles 3 and 3A, subject to exceptions and circumstances |
| Retention review | Flag an absent or due operator-set review date | NZ principle 9; Australian APP 11.2 where the organisation is an APP entity |
| Security review | Flag an absent or over ninety-day-old recorded review | Local review cadence supporting NZ principle 5 and Australian APP 11.1 |
| Judge declaration | Block scoring until a declaration is clear and evidence is recorded | Programme policy, not a statutory rule |
| Eligibility and complete panel | Block a decision unless the entry is submitted, eligible and fully judged by the required panel | Programme policy, not a statutory rule |

Primary sources:

- [NZ principle 3: collection from the individual](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/3/).
- [NZ principle 3A: indirect collection](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/3a/). An imported entry requires assessment; an import is not proof that notice obligations are met.
- [NZ principle 5: storage and security](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/).
- [NZ principle 9: retention](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/). Keep personal information only as long as a lawful purpose requires.
- [OAIC APP 11 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information). Where applicable, reasonable security measures and destruction or de-identification obligations include relevant exceptions and recordkeeping requirements.

Use privacy-review to record the notice assessment and next retention review. Use security-review to record evidence of an access and backup review. Neither command verifies that the underlying work happened. There is no automatic deletion or notice sending. The owner determines applicable law, purposes, holds, review dates and lawful disposal. Exports, activity details, paperwork, drafts, backups and separately held files also need access and retention controls.

The base does not handle entry fee collection, regulated payments or tax. Conflict rules and panel sizes are programme settings, not legal conclusions. A declaration is a human assertion. The chair resolves disputed eligibility, ties, bias and result approval.
